Build Your First Korean Syllables

Learn how Korean consonants and vowels come together to create meaningful syllables, words and sentences.

How Korean Syllables Work

Korean letters are combined into square-shaped syllable blocks. Every syllable has at least one consonant and one vowel. Some syllables also have a final consonant.

Let's Build a Syllable!
